Server Guidelines

SMP RULES & CONDUCT

To ensure a safe, fair, and immersive experience for all players, these rules are strictly enforced by the Moon Community staff team.

Treat all members with courtesy and kindness. Harassment, bullying, hate speech, threats, discrimination of any kind, and targeted toxicity are strictly prohibited under zero-tolerance enforcement.
Do not share NSFW, sexually explicit, or disturbing media/text. Keep public chat clean: spamming, excessive caps, flooding chat with repetitive messages, or intentionally derailing conversations is disallowed.
Promoting third-party Minecraft servers, social media, discord links, unapproved services, phishing links, or running suspicious schemes in chat or DMs will result in an immediate permanent ban.
Always comply with reasonable instructions issued by staff members. Never pretend to be staff, moderators, or another community player through names, skins, or misleading chat messages.
The use of hacked clients, kill aura, speed, fly, X-ray texture packs/mods, baritone, auto-clickers, or any modification that yields an unfair advantage over vanilla players is strictly forbidden.
Exploiting duplication bugs, boundary glitches, chunk loaders to cause unintended behavior, or any unintended server mechanic is prohibited. Report newly discovered bugs via support tickets immediately.
Destroying, defacing, modifying other players' creations or taking items from their containers without explicit permission is considered griefing and will result in rollbacks and punishments.
All in-game agreements, barters, and economy trades must be honored in good faith. Agreeing to trade and reneging after receiving items is punishable as fraud.
Using alternate accounts to bypass limits, stack starter resources, manipulate the in-game economy, or evade server restrictions is strictly disallowed.
Do not place or claim your own bounty through yourself, an alternate account, or a colluding accomplice to generate unearned wealth or exploit bounty reward systems.
Building intentional lag machines, cascading entity loops, unconstrained redstone clocks, or executing actions designed to lower server TPS or crash the instance is forbidden.
Joining on another account, using a VPN, changing IPs, or utilizing alternate Discord accounts to bypass mutes or bans will escalate penalties to permanent IP-wide blacklisting.
Never ask for, leak, or share private personal information (doxxing) or account credentials. You are solely responsible for protecting your own account login.
Do not argue staff actions or report rule-breakers in public chat. Open a ticket in our official Discord with clear evidence (screenshots/video) for professional, private review.
Enforcement & Punishment Hierarchy

Infractions are evaluated according to severity, intent, and player history:

1. Warning 2. Chat Mute 3. Server Kick 4. Temporary Ban 5. Permanent Blacklist
